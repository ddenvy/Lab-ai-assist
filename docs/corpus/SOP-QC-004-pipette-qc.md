## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Goal
This procedure describes the gravimetric check of mechanical and electronic pipettes, performed once per quarter.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Coverage
It applies to all laboratory pipettes with volumes from 10 to 1000 µL. Multichannel pipettes are covered by a separate procedure.

## 3. Frequency

### 3.1 Schedule
The check is performed at least once every 3 months, and also after a pipette is dropped, repaired, or suspected of inaccuracy. A pipette without a valid check must not be used.

## 4. Method

### 4.1 Weighing
Perform 10 weighings of the tested volume of deionized water on an analytical balance. Water temperature must be 20–25 °C; a density correction is applied in the calculation. The weighing series is recorded in the check protocol.

### 4.2 Calculation
Systematic error is calculated as the deviation of the mean volume from the nominal value. Random error is calculated as the RSD of the ten weighings.

## 5. Acceptance Criteria

### 5.1 Error limits
For a nominal volume of 1000 µL: systematic error no more than ±0.8%, RSD no more than 0.3%. For 100 µL: no more than ±1.0% and 0.5%. For 10 µL: no more than ±2.5% and 1.0%.

## 6. Actions on Non-Conformance

### 6.1 Out of limits
The pipette is withdrawn from use and sent for repair or written off. Analysis results from the period since the last check are reviewed for pipette influence.
