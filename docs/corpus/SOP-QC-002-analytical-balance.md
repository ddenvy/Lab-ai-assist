## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Goal
This procedure describes the daily check of analytical balances before weighing begins. The check confirms that balance readings remain within tolerance since the last calibration.

## 2. Scope

### 2.1 Coverage
The procedure applies to all laboratory analytical balances with 0.1 mg readability: Mettler Toledo MS204TS, Sartorius MSA225S. It does not apply to precision balances.

## 3. Responsibilities

### 3.1 Balance operator
The balance operator performs the check before the first weighing of the day and records the result in the balance log. The person responsible for metrology tracks external calibration deadlines.

## 4. Verification and Calibration

### 4.1 External calibration
External calibration is performed by an accredited organization at least once a year using E2-class weights. The date of the next calibration is shown on the balance plate; overdue balances must not be used.

## 5. Daily Check

### 5.1 Procedure
Perform two weighings of a 200 g test weight (F1 class) before and after zero adjustment. Record readings to 0.1 mg precision. If any point falls outside the tolerance, the check is repeated after an internal balance calibration.

## 6. Acceptance Criteria

### 6.1 Reading tolerance
Deviation of the reading from the nominal value of the 200 g weight — no more than ±0.5 mg.

### 6.2 Repeatability
Range of five repeated weighings of a 100 g weight — no more than 0.3 mg.

## 7. Actions on Non-Conformance

### 7.1 Removal from service
If the tolerance is not met, the balance is removed from service and the weighings obtained that day are reviewed. A record of the failure is entered in the balance log with the date and reason; calibration is called in.
